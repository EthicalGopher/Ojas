# Brawler video prompts

Start frame (image-to-video): `brawler_golden_full.png` (or `brawler_night_full.png` for the night version).
Character reference: `brawler_concept_full.jpg` (left figure). Do not add the sheet's title text.

## Main prompt (image-to-video, 8-10 s)

Cinematic painterly 3D animation, hand-painted textures with visible brush strokes, a stylised graphic-novel look.
A tough young female street brawler stands in a hazy industrial undercity at golden hour. She has tan skin, a
scowl, heavy dark brows, amber eyes, smoky dark eye make-up and fresh scratch marks across her right cheek. Her
black undercut is shaved on her left side, and the long top is swept over to her right, falling to her jaw with
a bright lime-green streak at the front. She wears a slate-grey crew-neck tank top under a weathered brown
leather harness with brass buckles and rivets, a wide belt with a brass buckle plus a slung belt, baggy olive
cargo trousers with flap pockets and riveted iron knee pads, and tall dark lace-up combat boots. Her right hand
and forearm are wrapped in off-white bandages. Her left arm is a heavy mechanical prosthetic: rusted cast-iron
segments, copper pipes running from a scar on her shoulder, an armoured iron hand, and glass vials of glowing
teal chemical liquid.

Action: she slowly lifts her head and looks into the camera, then clenches her mechanical fist. The teal vials
pulse brighter, the glow spills teal light across her harness, hip and face, and sparks of steam escape the
pipes. She drops into a boxing stance, fists up, and lets a slight smirk show.

Camera: a slow push-in from full body to a medium close-up, low angle, shallow depth of field.

Lighting: a hard warm amber key light from the side through a window, deep cool violet-blue shadows, a warm
red-orange glow where her skin turns from light to shadow, strong coloured rim light (warm on one side, cool on
the other), crisp painted highlights on her cheekbone, nose and hair, the teal glow from the arm lighting nearby
surfaces, bloom, volumetric haze with a light shaft behind her, out-of-focus bokeh lights in the background,
dust particles drifting in the light. High contrast, rich saturated colour, moody and cinematic.

## Night version (swap the lighting paragraph)

Lighting: night in a neon-lit undercity alley. A low warm pink-orange key light, deep blue-violet shadows,
magenta and blue rim lights, the teal glow from her arm as the brightest light source spilling across her body,
wet reflective ground, purple volumetric fog, neon bokeh (pink, blue, teal) behind her. High contrast, moody.

## Short prompt (for tools with a character limit)

Painterly 3D animated film still coming to life: a scowling tan female brawler with a black undercut and lime
streak, leather harness, olive cargo trousers, iron knee pads, combat boots, bandaged right hand and a rusted
mechanical left arm with glowing teal vials. She looks up, clenches her metal fist (the vials flare teal, steam
hisses) and raises her guard. Slow push-in, low angle. Hard amber side light, violet shadows, warm skin
terminator, coloured rim light, teal glow spill, bloom, hazy light shaft, bokeh, high contrast.

## Negative prompt

photorealistic, live action, plastic 3D look, flat lighting, low contrast, washed out, blurry face, extra
fingers, extra limbs, deformed hands, the mechanical arm switching sides, the bandage switching hands, changing
hairstyle, text, title, watermark, logo, subtitles.

## Settings tips

- Image-to-video with the start frame gives the most consistent character. Upload the concept sheet as an
  extra character reference if the tool allows it.
- 16:9 for a trailer shot, 9:16 for phone and reels; 24 fps; 8-10 s.
- If the arm or the bandage drifts to the wrong side, repeat in the prompt: "mechanical LEFT arm, bandaged
  RIGHT hand".
