## Tips

- Keep the character description identical in every clip (it is what keeps her the same from clip to clip).
- If the iron arm or the bandage swaps sides, add: "her LEFT arm is the iron prosthetic, her RIGHT hand is bandaged".
- If a fight looks like a video game (side-on, health bars, fighters squared up), add: "filmed like a live-action action movie, handheld camera, no on-screen UI".
- If teal light shows up anywhere else (her right hand, the room, the opponent), repeat: "teal light only from the device on her LEFT forearm and hand".
- If Flow makes her photoreal, move the "Stylized painterly 3D animated film" sentence to the start of the prompt.
- Clip 7 must end on a still, centred, full-body hold: the app takes the film's last frame as her still and plate
  (her 3D model then stands on that spot after the film).
